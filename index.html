(0) About

Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Boston University, supervised by Prof. Vijaya Kolachalama. Developing and deploying pathways to accelerate commercial adoption of translation-ready AI across science, medicine, and human-machine interaction.

Resume
(0.1) Bio

Prior to BU, Shuyue received an M.Phil. in Computer Science (2023) from City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Shiqi Wang, and a B.Eng. in Intelligence Science and Technology (2020) from Northeast Electric Power University, supervised by Prof. Yimin Hou. His Ph.D. research builds trustworthy autonomous AI systems grounded in real-world evidence — spanning retrieval, attributional, modality/sequence, temporal, and physical/spatial grounding — with the long-term goal of Safe, Extensible, and Cost-efficient (SEC) AGI for science, medicine, human-machine interaction, and robotics.

(4.1) Code

I prioritize research transparency and reproducibility, and provide links to the code and data associated with my previous research projects whenever possible. Exceptions include proprietary clinical data from hospitals such as BMC, LHMC, MGB, and BSH, as well as restricted-access datasets from NACC and NEJM.
